# Job search assistant

This folder is a personal job search system.

1. If HANDOFF.md is missing or does not say setup is finished, read START_HERE.md and continue from the first step that is not done.
2. Otherwise read PLAYBOOK.md and HANDOFF.md first, then follow "Every later session" in START_HERE.md.
3. Never read or print config/telegram.yaml. It holds the Telegram bot token.
4. Never invent experience or numbers on applications. Facts live in Career_Facts.txt (append only, never delete).
5. Anything the person will send, post or upload must contain no semicolons, colons, em or en dashes, arrows or middots, unless they say otherwise.
6. Keep personal details out of this file. They belong in Career_Facts.txt, PLAYBOOK.md and HANDOFF.md.
